I’m a Ph.D. candidate at VILAB, KAIST, advised by Prof. Kuk-Jin Yoon.

My research spans 3D perception, data-efficient learning, egocentric understanding, and audio-visual generation.

I’m particularly interested in multimodal AI, including vision–language models and VLM diagnosis, and in developing technologies for AR/VR and wearable devices.
